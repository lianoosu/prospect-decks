Starting assumptions, to validate with you

What we typically hear from Market Intelligence teams in travel retail

The ambition

  • A single, trusted view of brand and category performance across airports, regions and retailers
  • Spot shifts early: passenger flows, nationality mix, competitor moves, pricing
  • Get insight to commercial and brand teams faster, without a long analyst queue

What's often in the way

  • Data arrives from many places: retailer sell-out, internal sales, syndicated panels, passenger and travel data
  • Different formats and timings mean a lot of manual spreadsheet stitching
  • Answering "why did this change?" means going back and forth across sources

What must be true

  • Numbers the business trusts, with one definition of each KPI
  • Partner and retailer data shared securely under the right access
  • Easy enough for non-technical users to self-serve

These are our assumptions, not facts about Shiseido. We'd love you to correct them.
