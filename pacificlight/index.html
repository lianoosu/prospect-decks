Addressing the CII question

OT data reaches analytics without exposing the plant

Your OT systems stay on-premise. Data only flows outward, one way. No inbound connection ever reaches Level 0–2, and Snowflake never sends commands to the control system.

Plant OT zone (on-prem)

  • DCS / PLC / sensors (Levels 0–2)
  • Plant historian (Level 3)
  • Unchanged. Nothing is installed here.

OT DMZ (Level 3.5)

  • Historian replica / data diode (one-way)
  • Openflow runtime, outbound HTTPS only
  • Tag filtering: export only approved tags

Network path

  • Private connectivity (Private Link) to Snowflake
  • TLS in transit, network policies, key-pair or OAuth auth
  • Customer-managed keys (Tri-Secret Secure) available

Snowflake (region of your choice)

  • An isolated OT database, read-only for analytics roles
  • Row and column masking; full access history
  • Results flow back to engineers, never into the DCS

Your CSA (Cyber Security Agency of Singapore) CII obligations remain PacificLight's to manage. In a workshop, we would map this design against your CII cybersecurity code of practice and internal OT policy before any POC.

External data with no pipelines to build

Snowflake Marketplace: add outside data to your own models

Providers share live datasets straight into your account, so there is no FTP and no ETL. They join with your own data immediately, and they drive the price-modelling and load-forecast use cases.

Weather

Historical and forecast temperature, humidity, irradiance and rainfall. These drive load, solar output and gas-turbine ambient derating.

Fuel & carbon

LNG is PacificLight's main fuel, so its benchmark prices matter most. S&P Global publishes Platts LNG data, including the JKM benchmark, on Marketplace, alongside Brent, FX and carbon data. Together these drive fuel cost, spark spread and hedge decisions.

Macro & demographic

Economic indicators, business activity and holiday calendars. These drive C&I demand and churn propensity.

Firmographics

Company size, industry and growth signals. These improve C&I prospecting and credit-risk pricing.

Your own feeds

EMC/USEP data and vendor feeds (e.g. ICE, broker curves) loaded once and shared across teams through governed views.
